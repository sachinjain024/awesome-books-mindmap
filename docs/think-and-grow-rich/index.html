About This Book

Think and Grow Rich is one of the best-selling self-help books of all time. First published in 1937 after Napoleon Hill spent more than twenty years studying the habits, philosophies, and methods of over five hundred of the most successful people of his era — including Andrew Carnegie, Henry Ford, Thomas Edison, Theodore Roosevelt, and John D. Rockefeller — the book distills their collective wisdom into thirteen actionable principles for achieving success and wealth.

Hill’s central argument is both simple and radical: riches begin with a state of mind. The person who succeeds does so not primarily because of superior education, talent, or connections, but because of a definite mental attitude combined with a burning desire, persistent action, and an organized plan. The physical world, Hill argues, is the offspring of the mental world — and therefore transforming one’s circumstances begins with transforming one’s thoughts.

The thirteen principles presented in this book form a complete philosophy of achievement. They work not in isolation but as an integrated system: desire ignites the fire, faith sustains it, auto-suggestion fuels it, specialized knowledge channels it, imagination shapes it, organized planning structures it, decision launches it, persistence carries it through obstacles, and the Master Mind alliance amplifies it beyond what any individual could accomplish alone. The final three chapters — on the subconscious mind, the brain as a broadcasting station, and the sixth sense — bring the philosophy to its metaphysical apex.

This mind map presents all thirteen principles in structured, accessible chapters. Each chapter explains the principle in depth, includes the specific exercises and formulas Hill prescribes, and distills the key takeaways into memorable bullet points. Whether you are approaching this book for the first time or returning to it after years, this guide will help you internalize its timeless wisdom and translate it into concrete action.

Whatever the mind can conceive and believe, it can achieve. — Napoleon Hill
THINK AND GROW RICH
13 Principles for Wealth, Success & Achievement
THE FOUNDATION OF RICHES
Chapter 1
Desire: The Starting Point of All Achievement
All achievement begins with a burning desire — not a wish or a hope, but an obsessive, consuming fire that refuses to be extinguished. Hill outlines the six-step method for transforming desire into its financial equivalent through definite purpose and daily autosuggestion.
Chapter 2
Faith: Visualization and Belief in Attainment
Faith is the head chemist of the mind — it transmutes thought into its physical equivalent. By repeatedly mixing emotion with thought, you condition your subconscious to act upon your desire as if it were already real. The chapter explains how to develop unshakeable belief through affirmation and self-talk.
Chapter 3
Auto-Suggestion: The Medium for Influencing the Subconscious
Auto-suggestion is the agency of communication between the conscious and subconscious mind. You feed the subconscious mind only thoughts charged with emotion and belief. Hill explains the exact procedure for using repetition and feeling to plant the seed of desire deep in the subconscious.
KNOWLEDGE AND IMAGINATION
Chapter 4
Specialized Knowledge: Personal Experiences or Observations
General knowledge is of little value in accumulating wealth. What matters is specialized knowledge organized and intelligently directed through practical plans of action. Hill distinguishes between raw information and actionable expertise, and explains how to acquire and leverage the right kind of knowledge.
Chapter 5
Imagination: The Workshop of the Mind
The imagination is literally the workshop wherein are fashioned all plans created by man. Hill explains two forms of imagination — synthetic and creative — and argues that the ability to combine old ideas in new arrangements is the root of all invention and entrepreneurship.
PLANNING AND ACTION
Chapter 6
Organized Planning: The Crystallization of Desire into Action
Desire must be translated into definite, organized plans. Hill provides the blueprint for creating a Master Mind alliance, assembling a personal cabinet, and developing practical plans for accumulating wealth. This chapter covers leadership qualities, causes of failure, and the 31 major causes why people don't succeed.
Chapter 7
Decision: The Mastery of Procrastination
Analysis of 25,000 men and women revealed that lack of decision was near the top of the list of the 30 major causes of failure. People who accumulate great wealth are known as quick and definite decision-makers. This chapter explores how decisiveness and independence of thought are prerequisites for achievement.
Chapter 8
Persistence: The Sustained Effort Necessary to Induce Faith
Persistence is to the character of man what carbon is to steel. Without it, no amount of desire, planning, or specialized knowledge will produce results. Hill analyzes the four steps to developing persistence and profiles the individuals throughout history who owed their success entirely to their refusal to quit.
THE MASTER MIND AND BEYOND
Chapter 9
Power of the Master Mind: The Driving Force
Power is essential for accumulating money — and power is acquired through organized and intelligently directed knowledge. The Master Mind principle describes the coordination of knowledge and effort between two or more people, in a spirit of harmony, toward a definite purpose. This coordination creates a form of intelligence greater than any individual.
Chapter 10
The Mystery of Sex Transmutation
The emotion of sex contains the most powerful of all mind stimuli. When harnessed and redirected, this creative energy becomes the fuel for genius. Hill argues that the most successful men and women in history learned to transmute sexual energy into productive creative force, heightening imagination, courage, and persistence.
Chapter 11
The Subconscious Mind: The Connecting Link
The subconscious mind works day and night, receives and files impressions, and can be voluntarily directed through the seven major positive emotions. Hill explains how to communicate your desires to the subconscious through faith, desire, and auto-suggestion — and how to protect it from the seven negative emotions.
Chapter 12
The Brain: A Broadcasting and Receiving Station for Thought
Every human brain is both a broadcasting station and a receiving set for the vibration of thought. Through the Creative Imagination, the mind can tune in to thought vibrations from other minds and from Infinite Intelligence. Hill discusses how the Master Mind group amplifies mental broadcasting power beyond what any individual can achieve alone.
Chapter 13
The Sixth Sense: The Door to the Temple of Wisdom
The Sixth Sense is the apex of Hill's philosophy — a faculty of the mind that, through meditation and mastery of the other twelve principles, opens a channel to Infinite Intelligence. It is the point at which the subconscious mind touches the Universal Mind. This chapter describes the council of imaginary advisors and the final synthesis of all thirteen principles.

Core Stoic Principles

  • Burning Desire: The starting point of all achievement is a definite, obsessive desire — not a wish, but a consuming fire. You must know exactly what you want, commit fully, and refuse to accept defeat as anything other than temporary.
  • Faith and Autosuggestion: Faith is a state of mind induced by repeated instruction to the subconscious through affirmation and visualization. By mixing emotion with repeated thought, you persuade your subconscious to act on your desire as though it were already real.
  • Specialized Knowledge: It is not enough to know things in general. Wealth is created through the organized application of specialized knowledge directed toward a definite purpose. Knowing how to find and apply knowledge is more valuable than possessing it.
  • The Master Mind: No individual has sufficient experience, education, or knowledge to succeed alone. A Master Mind alliance — two or more people working in harmony toward a common goal — creates a combined mental power greater than the sum of its parts.
  • Persistence: Most people give up when resistance first appears. Persistence is the trait that separates those who accumulate great wealth from those who merely dream of it. Combined with a burning desire and a definite plan, persistence becomes an irresistible force.
  • Sex Transmutation: The most powerful human emotion, when redirected toward creative work, produces a heightened state of mind that drives invention, leadership, and extraordinary achievement. The great creative geniuses of history channeled this energy consciously.
